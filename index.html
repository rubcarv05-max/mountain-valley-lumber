About Mountain Valley Lumber

We're a locally operated sawmill and home-building business based in Saguache, Colorado.

We cut lumber and build homes for people in our part of the state, and we take the job personally. When you call, you reach the people doing the work. When we take on a project, we see it through with care.

  • Craftsmanship

    Careful, skilled work at every step, from the first cut to the finished building.

  • Quality materials

    Good lumber is the foundation of good building. We pay attention to what goes into every job.

  • Personal service

    Straight answers and direct conversations with the people doing the work.

  • Pride in the work

    We put our name on what we make, and we want it to last.
